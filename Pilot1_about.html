About NutriBoost Z1

NutriBoost Z1 is a daily nutritional supplement developed by Aurora Wellness Labs to support balanced nutrition and overall wellness. The supplement is designed to provide essential vitamins and minerals that contribute to immune health, energy metabolism, and cognitive wellness.

NutriBoost Z1 incorporates Aurora Wellness Labs' proprietary BioFusion-7 Matrix technology and Aurora Nutrient Stabilization Process. These technologies are designed to support ingredient consistency and maintain nutrient quality throughout the product's shelf life.

Each capsule contains a carefully selected combination of vitamins, minerals, and nutritional compounds intended to support adult wellness. NutriBoost Z1 is designed for adults aged 18 years and above and is manufactured according to internal quality-control procedures established by Aurora Wellness Labs.

Product Claims

  • Supports immune health and daily wellness
  • Supports normal energy metabolism
  • Supports cognitive wellness and focus
  • Provides antioxidant protection

Recommended Usage

24 months from the manufacturing date when stored according to the recommended storage conditions.

Shelf Life

24 months from the date of manufacture when stored according to the recommended storage conditions.

Storage Conditions

Store below 25°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not consume if the container is damaged or if the tamper-evident seal is broken.

Proprietary Technology

NutriBoost Z1 utilizes BioFusion-7 Matrix and the Aurora Nutrient Stabilization Process. These proprietary technologies were developed by Aurora Wellness Labs to support ingredient stability, consistency, and product quality throughout normal storage conditions.
